## Deliberately left in the backlog

**Packaging — not in v1.1.**
`2026-08-01-extract-the-vice-mcp-into-an-installable-package.md` (major) and
`2026-08-01-pack-the-whole-c64-re-toolkit-including-the-vice-mcp-into-one-package.md` (major).

Both are real and both are *reuse* goals: they make the MCP consumable by other projects. Nothing
in Phase 1's remaining play-through or Phases 2–4 is blocked by their absence. v1.1's bar is
"live emulator work survives its own failure modes", and packaging does not move it. Sequencing
argument on top of that: 01.6 relocates a large fraction of the broker into Node and changes the
proxy↔broker protocol, so packaging the tree *before* that lands means packaging a shape that is
about to change. These belong after v1.1, and 01.6's outcome is an input to them.

**Cross-project broker — split 2026-08-02, only its blocker came into v1.1.**
`2026-08-01-make-the-broker-cross-project-via-shared-home-dir-state.md` (filed `minor`).

The first pass deferred this whole todo. That was wrong in one respect, corrected here: **it
carries `01.2-REVIEW.md`'s CR-01, an independently-verified blocker**, and the todo itself argues
*"fixing CR-01 and going cross-project are the same piece of work, not two."* CR-01 is the absence
of a singleton guard on `vice-broker.sh start` — two concurrent brokers race in `grant_from_spare()`
and can hand one ready spare to two sessions. That is a live correctness hole in the isolation
Phase 01.2 exists to provide, and it does not become less true by being filed under a `minor`
heading about convenience.

Disposition:

- **CR-01 → Phase 01.6, criterion 11. Required.** A TCP listener on a well-known port cannot bind
  twice, so the guard is close to free under the new design — but "close to free" is a hypothesis
  to test, not a fix to assume.
- **Cross-project reach → Phase 01.6, criterion 12. Explicitly optional** (developer, 2026-08-02:
  *"we might get that for free with the new system. If we don't get it it can be ignored."*). The
  transport stops being workspace-scoped the moment it is TCP; the only thing still scoped is the
  bootstrap `broker.json`. So the question collapses to "does one file move?" rather than the six
  directories of protocol state the todo had to contemplate.
- **The rest of the todo stays in the backlog** — ownership metadata, the `manually_started`
  never-reap marker, the reaper rule, and revisiting D-1.2-D's `0600`/uid-parity posture for a
  `$HOME` path. Same reasoning as packaging: it rewrites state paths 01.6 is about to relocate, and
  doing both at once makes a regression impossible to attribute.

Also carried into 01.6's criterion 11 from this todo: the unexplained port-6510 teardown recorded
as NOT CAPTURED in `01.2-CRITERION-13-EVIDENCE.md` §5/§7. A second broker adopting state is exactly
what that would look like, and it is worth revisiting once a guard exists.

**Likely closeable without work.**
`collapse-vice-selector-skill-into-proxy.md` (medium) carries its own `STALE 2026-08-01` banner:
`.claude/skills/vice-mcp-selector/` was deleted outright in `db9eed3` rather than collapsed, the
two assertions reading its SKILL.md were repointed at CLAUDE.md in `e0f9915`, and the CLAUDE.md
skills-table row is gone. Its own note says "this may be closeable as-is." Verify and close rather
than scheduling it.
