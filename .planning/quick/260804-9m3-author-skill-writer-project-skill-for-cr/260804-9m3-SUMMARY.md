# Quick Task 260804-9m3 — Summary

skill in this repo. Eight sections: the frontmatter contract, where files go,

to copy.

Registered in `.claude/CLAUDE.md`'s Project Skills table, along with `find-skills`,

## Why it was authored rather than installed

`npx skills find` surfaced no credible skill-creation skill. The only
`skill-creator` match carries 10 installs; the rest were unrelated
(`creator-registry`, `component-scaffolder`). Authoring locally also lets the skill
carry this repo's hard rules, which no ecosystem skill would know.

## Deviation from plan — Task 2

**Planned:** register the skill by running `gsd-tools generate-claude-md`, on the
reasoning that the table is generated and hand-editing would be discarded.

**What happened:** the generator regenerates *every* managed block, not just
`skills`, from `.planning/PROJECT.md` and `.planning/research/STACK.md` — both stale
relative to CLAUDE.md. The one-row change came out as 62 insertions / 36 deletions
and regressed three constraints: the `mcp__vice__*` single-route rule was weakened
into a form citing a `devcontainer-host-path` skill that no longer exists, the
headless-container constraint was deleted outright, and the settled
`.d64`-from-Python decision reverted to "needs a solution chosen during research".

**Resolution:** reverted with `git checkout`, confirmed both hard rules intact by
`grep`, and added the two table rows by hand. Final `.claude/CLAUDE.md` diff is
2 insertions, confined to the `GSD:skills-*` block.

The hazard is filed as
`.planning/todos/pending/2026-08-04-generate-claude-md-regresses-hand-refined-constraints.md`
rather than fixed inline — reconciling PROJECT.md and STACK.md against CLAUDE.md is
a docs task with its own review surface. The skill's § Register it documents the
carve-out and the diff-before-commit guard, and the todo's step 5 removes that
carve-out once the sources are reconciled.

This deviation changed the skill's content, not just the mechanics: § Register it
now teaches the opposite of what the plan assumed, which is the more valuable
version.
