# Phase 01.3 Plan 02: vice_diagnose Checkpoint-Trap Check and Cycle Bracket Summary

**A read-mostly `vice_diagnose` MCP tool answering which of five states (restarted, checkpoint_trap, wedged, stale_read_path, live) a session's emulator is in, checking a self-inflicted checkpoint trap before ever resuming the machine, then falling back to a single-definition cycle bracket that never trusts `vice_ping`'s execution field or a manufactured degradation threshold.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** single session
- **Tasks:** 2/2 completed
- **Files modified:** 3 (2 code, 1 RE-FINDINGS.md addendum)

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 (checkpoint-trap check):** Added `DIAGNOSE_TOOL` (proxy-local, never in `tools-manifest.json`, dispatched before the deny-list check exactly like `vice_recycle`), the closed `DIAGNOSE_VERDICTS` vocabulary, `resolveLiveIrqHandler()` (the single `$01`/`$0314`/`$FFFE` vector lookup, resolved fresh on every call, memoising nothing), and `handleDiagnose()` answering two of the five states without ever resuming the machine: `restarted` from a plain epoch-file comparison at zero emulator cost, and `checkpoint_trap` from enumerated checkpoints plus the resolved handler, on two named shapes (an armed stopping exec checkpoint exactly at the current PC, or one at the resolved handler entry with `hit_count` exactly 0). The trap report explains and never remedies (D-15), naming the agent's own next moves without performing any of them, and closes with the not-guaranteed paragraph citing the recorded incident where checkpoint delete, a soft reset, a hard reset, and an explicit single step all left the machine frozen.
- **Task 2 (the cycle bracket):** Added `CYCLE_BRACKET_PINGS`/`CYCLE_BRACKET_MAX`/`BASELINE_CYCLES_PER_SECOND`, `runCycleBracket()` (the single reset/resume-once/poll-x3/pause/read definition, no wall-clock pacing construct anywhere in it), `classifyLiveness()` (two consecutive zero brackets is `wedged` and nothing else is; a non-zero bracket with byte-identical registers across it is `stale_read_path`; anything else is `live`), and `renderDiagnoseReport()` (separates load-bearing evidence — the restart epoch, the stopwatch delta — from corroborating evidence — PC, VIC-II state, checkpoint hit counts, screenshot — explicitly, and prints a below-baseline non-zero rate purely as an observation beside the ~991,000/s baseline, never as its own verdict). Wired into `handleDiagnose()` after the trap check, completing D-14's full order: epoch, trap, bracket.
- Two structural guards added: the cycle bracket's single-definition invariant (confirmed to fire — a temporary stray `vice_cycles_stopwatch` call outside `runCycleBracket()` broke the test, then was reverted before this commit), and `DIAGNOSE_VERDICTS`' closed five-member vocabulary.
- Logged a reverse-engineering finding to `.planning/RE-FINDINGS.md`: the specific bit of `$01` (HIRAM, bit 1, mask `0x02`) that decides which IRQ vector pair is live, sharpening the existing MEDIUM-confidence "vector table" entry which named the check but not the bit.
- **244/244 tests pass** across the whole `.claude/mcp/vice/*.test.mjs` suite (231 baseline + 13 new), no flake observed on the final run.

## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in frontmatter. In brief: the checkpoint-trap check recognizes two distinct shapes (at-PC and at-resolved-handler-with-zero-hits), matching the two different incident signatures on record; the HIRAM bit interpretation is logged as a doc-derived RE finding rather than assumed silently; the reported cycles-per-second rate is measured via real elapsed time purely for an observational print, which is a different concern from the project's standing prohibition on wall-clock *pacing*; and `gatherWedgeEvidence()`/`CHECKPOINT_ARMING_TOOLS` (named in the phase-level artifact list) are correctly left to sibling plans 01.3-03/01.3-04 rather than introduced here.
