## Phase Goal

See `01-01-PLAN.md` § Phase Goal. ROADMAP's Phase 1 Goal line is not in user-story form and no story was invented.

<objective>
Settle whether the two cracks are genuinely independent, and designate the canonical disassembly subject with a number rather than a preference.

Purpose: these two questions are joined at the hip. The independence verdict retroactively sets the weight of every agreement verdict in the ledger plan 01-05 just generated — if the two releases share a common ancestor, "both agree" means far less than it appears to, and every HIGH confidence in the ledger has to be reconsidered. And the canonical designation is the file that Phases 2, 3 and 4 index every address against, so the reason it was chosen has to survive a challenge, which means it has to be a measurement.

Output: `recovery/PROVENANCE.md` completed with the independence verdict and its tiered evidence, plus `recovery/clean/bruce-lee.bin`, its manifest, and `recovery/clean/README.md`.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| external scene records (CSDb) → provenance claims | Untrusted third-party prose becomes attribution evidence. Admissible for release lineage under D-15, but it can never outrank the binary, and it must never be mistaken for the third-party *byte data* REQUIREMENTS.md bans outright. |
| independence verdict → every agreement verdict in the ledger | Retroactive. If the cracks share an ancestor, every HIGH confidence the ledger assigned on the strength of agreement is overstated, and the ledger built in plan 01-05 needs reconsidering. |
| canonical designation → Phases 2, 3 and 4 | `recovery/clean/bruce-lee.bin` becomes the offset basis for the coverage bitmap, the checkpoint regions and the round-trip diff. A designation nobody can regenerate the reason for is a designation that cannot be defended when challenged. |
| registry `canonical` field → `recovery/clean/` projection | The directory must stay a projection of the field rather than becoming an independent privileged artifact, or the release-centric model silently reverts. |

**ASVS L1 applicability:** unchanged — no network service, no untrusted *input path* into executable behaviour, no auth, session, access-control or cryptographic-control surface. The one external data source in this plan is read-only prose consulted by a human and quoted with its tier. The modelled risk class is evidence integrity.
