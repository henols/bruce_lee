## Phase Goal

See `01-01-PLAN.md` § Phase Goal. ROADMAP's Phase 1 Goal line is not in user-story form and no story was invented.

<objective>
Normalise the recovered images to a common state and a *proven* base address, diff them, and turn the result into a ledger that gives every one of the 65536 addresses a verdict with its evidence and its confidence.

Purpose: this is the step that separates original Datasoft bytes from cracker patches, and it is the one most able to produce confident nonsense. An un-normalised diff manufactures false CRACKER-PATCH verdicts wholesale — relocation shifts every subsequent byte, so a one-byte offset error produces a ledger that is entirely wrong and entirely plausible. Everything downstream cites this document, so the arithmetic gets tests before it gets trusted.

Output: `tools/diff-images.mjs`, its unit tests, `recovery/PROVENANCE.md`, and both range manifests promoted from `ranges-only` to `bucketed`.
</objective>

Verb `anchor-search`; flags `--offset`, `--json`. Zero third-party dependencies: `Buffer.indexOf` and `node:crypto` are sufficient, per D-18.

`anchorSearch(source, target, {minRunLength, count})` selects several long, distinctive byte runs from the source image — long enough and varied enough that a coincidental match is implausible, and drawn from different regions rather than clustered — and locates each in the target with `Buffer.indexOf`. For every anchor it reports its source offset, *all* match offsets found in the target, the computed delta, and the target bytes at `delta-1`, `delta` and `delta+1`. Those three neighbour bytes are the point: an off-by-one in the accepted offset shifts every downstream verdict, so the boundary is printed rather than assumed.

`proveOffset(anchorResults)` accepts a single global offset only when every anchor's delta agrees, and rejects any anchor that matched at more than one target offset. On disagreement it fails with the disagreeing anchors named and their deltas listed, and the fallback is per-region offsets recorded in each release's range manifest and stated in the notes — never a majority vote, which is how a plausible-but-wrong ledger gets built. The expected outcome is a proven offset of zero, since the game must run at its own addresses, but "expected" is not "assumed": the search runs and the result is recorded either way.

Offset application is exact integer arithmetic. An address whose offset-adjusted counterpart falls outside `$0000`-`$FFFF` is recorded as out-of-range with a named reason and excluded from the compared set. It is never wrapped modulo 65536: a wrap silently compares two unrelated regions and reports the difference as a cracker patch.

Write `tools/diff-images.test.mjs` with `node:test` and `node:assert/strict` covering every behaviour above. Use small synthetic buffers for the arithmetic and boundary cases so the tests are fast and independent of the real dumps, and add one case that runs `anchorSearch` against the two real committed dumps to confirm the search finds unique anchors in practice.
