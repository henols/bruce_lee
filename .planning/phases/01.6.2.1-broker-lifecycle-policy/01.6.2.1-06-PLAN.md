frontmatters (every `verification: backstop` truth — 5 of them)**, plus `01.6.2-VERIFICATION.md`'s

claims. The planner may **refine but not narrow** it.

explicitly names.

a strict superset of CONTEXT's default — nothing is narrowed — and it is what makes completeness
auditable, which is the stated reason CONTEXT asked for a mechanical harvest in the first place. The
discrepancy is recorded here rather than resolved silently, per this project's own rule about
calibration figures.
