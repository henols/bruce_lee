<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** *a grantable instance*, not *a freshly launched instance*.

**Decision: `promote`.**

Today `handleAcquire()` has exactly one way to satisfy an acquire — cold launch — and exactly one
`state.grants.set()` call site, sitting immediately after `acquirePortAndLaunch()`. P-01 introduces
a second source of a grant. The correct move is to promote *"resolve a grantable instance"* to the
primary operation and demote *"cold launch"* to one of two ways of obtaining one — **not** to bolt a
warm-path branch alongside a still-primary cold path.

Consequence, and the reason this matters beyond tidiness: after the promotion there is still exactly
**one** grant-recording step, fed by both arms. That single call site is what the structural
anti-regression gate in task 2 can count. Defect 5's failure shape was *"correct module, never
called"*; a design with two independent grant-recording sites would let a future third arm
reintroduce it invisibly.

**Accepted debt:** none. No `add-alongside` shape is retained.
</assumption_delta_decision>
