<objective>
Complete the kill discipline with its corrected identity string, wire it to every catchable
shutdown path, and build the unconditional startup reap — derived from the emulator port band plus
process identity rather than from a registry the restart just lost.

Purpose: criterion D says the identity-verified kill is reused, not re-derived, and the ROADMAP is
explicit that the expected-identity substring needs correcting rather than copying — this broker
spawns the emulator directly and there is no intermediate process for the old expected string to
match. A wrong-but-compiling identity string makes every kill silently refuse while logging a
plausible pid-reuse warning, which is the real new risk this phase introduces. Criterion I is the
one place where deleting the on-disk registry costs something: with state in process, a restarted
broker has no record of what is running, and the seed already flagged that the reap must reach
instances it has never heard of.

Output: `broker-kill.mts` complete with its own test file, shutdown wired across every catchable
path, the unconditional startup reap, and the mandatory start-time banner.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| host process table → broker | Process listings and argument strings are untrusted input that decide which pids get killed |
| recorded pid → kill target | A pid recorded at spawn may have been recycled by the operating system before the kill |
| signal delivery → broker | Signals arrive at arbitrary times, including during a shutdown already in progress |

## STRIDE Threat Register

ASVS level 1. Blocking disposition on `high`.
