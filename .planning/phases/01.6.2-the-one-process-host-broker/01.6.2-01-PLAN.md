<objective>
Capture the live bash-written epoch and broker fixtures before anything can destroy them, take the
one-way decision on the control-plane wire format, and then prove the entire new topology end to
end on one path: authored TypeScript, compiled by `tsc` into banner-marked committed JavaScript,
deployed by the existing installer, container-guarded in TypeScript at process startup, binding a
TCP control listener with per-boot capability-token auth, serving one `acquire`, spawning one stub
child through the guarded launch seam, writing that child's `epoch.json`, and releasing plus
identity-verified-killing it when the connection closes.

Purpose: nine plans of work ride on this topology. Proving it on one path first means an
architectural dead-end costs one commit instead of ten. The fixture capture is first because
`01.6.2-VALIDATION.md` flags it as order-dependent and irreversible if missed — the bash broker is
running right now with four recorded instances, and once it is gone there is nothing concrete for
"the epoch contract is unchanged" to diff against.

Output: three frozen JSON fixtures with a provenance README; six new host-bound TypeScript
modules; an extended `vice-broker.mts` that is a long-lived process rather than a write-once
tracer; a minimal TCP client on the container side; an end-to-end test; grown build config; a
launcher that no longer owns the container guard.
</objective>
