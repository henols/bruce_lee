<objective>
Complete the broker's coordination core: the typed in-process state that replaces six on-disk
locations, port allocation re-banded to 6600, the three counts, and the launch path with its single
in-flight owner and its serialised warming. Deliver criterion C's required concurrency race test as
a real regression test for the 2026-08-01 triple-launch outage.

Purpose: C4 says the broker holds all its state in one place, in process. This is that place. And
the single in-flight owner is the guard that stopped three emulators being launched simultaneously;
it has to survive the move from a 500 ms poll loop to an event loop, deliberately, with a test that
would catch its loss.

Output: `broker-state.mts` and `broker-launch.mts` complete, each with its own test file, and the
broker's evaluation pass wired to call them in a fixed order.
