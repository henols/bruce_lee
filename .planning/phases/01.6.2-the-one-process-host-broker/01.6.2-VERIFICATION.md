### Gaps Summary

Two Critical defects, independently confirmed by direct grep and source read (not merely trusted
from `01.6.2-REVIEW.md`), mean the phase's own goal statement — "...owns coordination, per-instance
supervision and the warm pool..." — is not actually true of the wired system, despite a fully green
368-test suite:

1. **CR-01 (per-instance supervision never wired):** `broker-launch.mts`'s complete, unit-tested
   crash-respawn supervisor (`superviseChild()`) is dead code from the real broker's point of view.
   Both real launch paths in `vice-broker.mts` spawn without any exit observation. Consequence: a
   crashed or recycled `x64sc` process leaks its port and its capacity slot forever, and
   `vice_recycle` — a session-facing, documented tool — does not do what it says (it kills but never
   respawns). This is compounded by WR-01 (the `deliberateKill` marker is never set on release or
   recycle), which is currently silent only because CR-01 leaves no supervisor to misread it; fixing
   one without the other reintroduces a kill-never-recycle regression.

2. **CR-02 (disconnect-while-queued leak):** `attemptAcquire()`'s `socket.destroyed` check runs
   after the real spawn/grant side effect, not before. Ordinary client behavior (a timed-out or
   restarted session that was mid-acquire) triggers a real, permanently leaked emulator launch with
   no path to reclaim it.

Both defects are reachable by ordinary, non-adversarial behavior, both are provable from wiring
alone, and both directly undermine criterion A's own promise: `01.6.2-VALIDATION.md`'s 61-test
disposition ledger's row 51 explicitly claims the retiring bash suite's recycle-respawn assertion
is "RE-OBSERVED" by tests that, once CR-01 is accounted for, do not actually observe that behavior
in the shipped broker. That ledger inaccuracy is reported as its own gap (truth #7) because
criterion A's entire value is the ledger being an honest account of what changed — not merely a
row-count match.

Nothing here touches criteria E, L, or M, and nothing here contradicts the already-established
facts about C6 (one shell script), the deletion commit, the fixture preservation, or the 368-test
green suite count — the suite is genuinely green, and genuinely does not cover the two paths these
gaps live in.
