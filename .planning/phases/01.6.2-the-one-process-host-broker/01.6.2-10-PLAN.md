<objective>
Discharge criterion A's first half: a complete disposition ledger for every test in the retiring bash
daemon's test file, a complete function-by-function disposition for both retiring scripts, and any
replacement test a ledger row names but that nobody has written yet.

Purpose: the retiring suite verifies the daemon by spawning it as a subprocess and inspecting on-disk
files, and that mechanism stops existing entirely. Criterion A says it is redesigned rather than ported
and must be sized as its own work, never folded into a generic conversion line item — and this phase's
validation artifact already requires that every one of its tests carry a named disposition with no
silent drops. Roughly a third transfer as black-box tests against the new implementation, a third need
the new in-memory observation point, and a third have no port at all; the ledger is where that stops
being an estimate and becomes a record.

Output: two tables in the validation artifact, whatever replacement tests were missing, and reconciled
count arithmetic that plan 11 executes against.
</objective>

the test's name, what it actually exercises as read from its body rather than inferred from its name,

figure is sixty-one; a difference means something changed and needs investigating rather than
accepting. The number of ledger rows must equal the counted number.
