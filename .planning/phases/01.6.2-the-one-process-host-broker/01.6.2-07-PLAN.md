<objective>
Swap the proxy onto the TCP control plane and delete the file protocol's container half in the same
commit: acquire, release and recycle over one connection, the retiring client functions and their
constants gone, and the tests whose subjects went with them removed against plan 06's written
disposition.

Purpose: this is where the lease stops being a file and becomes a connection. Criterion F is explicit
that the six mechanisms retire together, because keeping any one leaves two competing authorities on
whether a lease is alive. Doing the swap and the deletion in one commit is what makes that literally
true rather than aspirational — a deletion one commit earlier breaks the typecheck, and one commit
later leaves a window where both authorities exist and neither is tested against the other.

Output: a proxy whose broker interaction is entirely over TCP, a client module with only its surviving
surface, and a test suite with no test left behind whose subject is gone.
</objective>
