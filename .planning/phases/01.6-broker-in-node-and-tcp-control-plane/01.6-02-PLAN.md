**Why this plan exists at all — a planner finding not present in any source artifact.** CONTEXT D-02
and the ROADMAP treat `vice-pool.test.mjs` as belonging entirely to the deleted subsystem. It does
not. Reading its 76 test titles shows it is a **mixed** file that accumulated coverage for four
surviving modules:

| Subject | Tests | Fate of the subject |
|---|---|---|
| `vice-pool.mjs` lease layer, `vice-session.mjs` sessions | ~45 | deleted (D-02) |
| `vice-probe.mjs` | 9 (lines 1172–1309) | **survives** |
| `repo-root.mjs` ladder and path agreement | 4 (lines 947–1091) | **survives** |
| `install-resources.mjs` | 12 (lines 1634–1782) | **survives, and plan 03 edits it** |
| `vice.mjs` `serverInfo()` deny-list stripping | 1 (line 857) | **survives** |
| `vice.mjs` CLI (`formatToolsOutput`, usage output, `pool status`) | 3 | deleted with the CLI (D-05) |
| `resources/vice-supervisor.sh` structural | 3 (lines 1807–1888) | **survives to 01.6.2** |

Deleting the file wholesale would delete the only coverage `install-resources.mjs` has — the module
criterion 9 rests on and the module plan 03 is about to modify. This plan rehomes what survives,
**before** plan 04 deletes anything. It is a pure addition: nothing is removed here.

**Duplication during waves 2 and 3 is expected and deliberate.** The rescued assertions exist in two
places until plan 04 deletes `vice-pool.test.mjs`. The suite count therefore *rises* by roughly 28
in this plan and falls by 76 in plan 04. Both numbers are recorded so neither reads as a regression.
Editing the 1,893-line original three times to remove each rescued block costs more than letting the
wholesale deletion take it.

**Rescue, do not transcribe.** Several assertions name subjects that are being deleted. The
path-agreement test in particular spawns `--print-paths` against `vice-pool.sh` and imports
`poolDir` and `sessionFilePath` in a child process; three of its four Node-side subjects go away.
Narrow it to the survivors and extend it to the launcher plan 01 created — a rewrite that keeps the
regression it was written to catch, not a copy that breaks in two waves' time.

**New tests are `.mjs`, not `.ts`.** Their subjects are still `.mjs` modules, and the typecheck
project runs with `allowJs` off, so a `.ts` test importing a `.mjs` module is a compile error. They
convert with their subjects in 01.6.1. See `01.6-01-PLAN.md` § planning notes for the convention.

</planning_notes>
